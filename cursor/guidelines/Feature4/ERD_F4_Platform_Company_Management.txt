RELATIONSHIP CARDINALITY:
================================================================================

1. Company 1..* UserRoleAssignment
   - Each company has many user role assignments
   - Company owns users through UserRoleAssignment

2. Company 1..* Employee
   - Each company has many employees
   - Employees must belong to a company (required)

3. Company 1..* Leaves
   - Each company has many leave records
   - Company-scoped domain record

4. Company 1..* Tasks
   - Each company has many tasks
   - Company-scoped domain record

5. Company 1..* Notifications
   - Each company has many notifications
   - Company-scoped domain record

================================================================================
NOTES:
- Company is the tenant organization and data isolation boundary
- Company owns all company-scoped records (leaves, tasks, salaries, notifications, etc.)
- Company owns Users via UserRoleAssignment
- Company owns Employees directly
- Only Primary Key (id) and Foreign Key fields are shown
- Business fields and audit fields are excluded per ERD rules
- Domain records (leaves, tasks, notifications) represent all company-scoped entities
