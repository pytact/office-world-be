|       Company        |   1     M   |      Employee        |   1     1   |        User          |

| id (PK)              |<----------->| id (PK)              |<----------->| id (PK)              |

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Company 1..* Employee: One Company has many Employees
- User 1..1 Employee: One User has exactly one Employee

NOTES:
- Employee table includes foreign keys to Company (from F-004) and User (from F-001)
- Only Primary Key (id) and Foreign Key fields are shown per db_instruction.md rules
- Business fields and audit fields are excluded from ERD
