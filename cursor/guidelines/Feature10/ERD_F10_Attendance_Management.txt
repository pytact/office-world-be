RELATIONSHIP CARDINALITY:
- Company 1..* Attendance (One Company has many Attendance records)
- Employee 1..* Attendance (One Employee has many Attendance records)
- Attendance 1..* AttendanceLog (One Attendance has many AttendanceLog entries)
- Employee 1..* AttendanceLog (One Employee has many AttendanceLog entries)

NOTES:
- Attendance table includes foreign keys to Company (from F-004) and Employee (from F-005)
- AttendanceLog table includes foreign keys to Attendance and Employee
- Only Primary Key (id) and Foreign Key fields are shown per db_instruction.md rules
- Business fields (attendance_date, check_in_time, check_out_time, status, worked_time, is_auto_check_out) are excluded
- Audit fields (created_at, updated_at, created_by, updated_by, deleted_at, deleted_by) are excluded
- Relationship: Exactly one Attendance per employee per day (enforced at application level)
